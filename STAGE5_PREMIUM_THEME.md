# Stage 5 — European Premium Hospitality Theme

This stage replaces plain white application surfaces with a cohesive hospitality-oriented visual system designed for professional portfolio presentation.

## Design language
- Deep navy/slate workspace surfaces with restrained contrast
- Azure, teal and warm-gold semantic accents
- Dark data grids optimized for long operational sessions
- Glass-inspired cards over the hotel artwork on the dashboard
- Consistent inputs, tabs, dialogs, status colors and hierarchy
- Persian RTL and English LTR behavior preserved

The goal is a calm, enterprise-grade PMS aesthetic rather than a decorative consumer UI.
